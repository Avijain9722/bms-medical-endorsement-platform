# Additional Inputs Required Before Production Completion

The current package is sufficient for discovery, workbook inspection and an initial prototype. The following additional inputs should be obtained before production completion:

1. At least three correctly completed examples for every portal template.
2. Rejected or incorrectly completed examples and insurer/TPA rejection messages.
3. Current client employee master sheets.
4. Current insured-member master sheets.
5. Principal-card and family-number mappings.
6. Category-to-salary-band mappings by client, entity and policy.
7. Category-to-contract and category-to-plan mappings.
8. Department, grade, subgroup and occupation lists where applicable.
9. Required-document checklist by transaction type and insurer/TPA.
10. Effective-date rules by client and policy.
11. Newborn processing rules by insurer/TPA.
12. Emirates ID placeholder rules and permitted use cases.
13. Supporting-document and ZIP naming rules.
14. Additional NextCare, MedNet, NAS, Daman, Sukoon and other portal templates.
15. BMS user-role matrix and approval authority.
16. BMS information-security, hosting, backup and retention requirements.
17. Expected daily case volume and maximum batch size.
18. Confirmation of the current approved BMS brand-guideline version.
19. Any approved web/UI design guidance from BMS Marketing.
